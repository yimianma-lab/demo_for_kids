What you're seeing

  • Trade winds — Equatorial heating creates a pressure gradient; winds flow toward the equator and are deflected by the Coriolis force (east in the tropics).
  • Ocean currents — Wind stress drives surface currents; Coriolis deflects them, forming subtropical gyres (e.g. North Atlantic).
  • Particles — Yellow trails show how water would move; try reducing Coriolis to see circulation weaken.

This is a minimal educational model, not a full climate simulation. Land blocks flow and helps form basin-scale gyres.
